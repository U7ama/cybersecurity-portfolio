# Professional Statement

My name is Hashim Aslam, and I am a Software Engineering student at COMSATS University Islamabad, Lahore Campus, with a long-term goal of building a career in cybersecurity.

My interest in this field grew out of my work as a developer. While building full-stack projects such as an e-commerce platform using React.js, FastAPI, and MongoDB, and a password manager using PostgreSQL, I found myself paying close attention to how these systems could be misused rather than just how they functioned. That shift in perspective, from building software to thinking about how it could be attacked and defended, is what drew me toward cybersecurity as a specialization.

Alongside my degree, I have taken a structured approach to building relevant skills outside the classroom. I am currently working through Google's Cybersecurity Professional Certificate and Princeton's Algorithms and Data Structures course, and I regularly practice Linux command-line fundamentals in a hands-on VS Code environment. I approach learning methodically, breaking down complex topics, documenting what I learn, and building small projects to reinforce the concepts rather than treating coursework as a checklist to complete.

Outside of technical work, I serve as Class Representative and Batch Ambassador for my department, roles that have required me to communicate clearly with faculty, coordinate between students, and take ownership of problems that affect a group rather than just myself. I see clear parallels between this kind of coordination and the collaborative, communication-heavy nature of security work, where technical findings need to be explained clearly to both technical and non-technical stakeholders.

My immediate goals are to complete a strong foundation in cybersecurity fundamentals, gain hands-on experience through an internship during my degree, and build a portfolio of real, documented work rather than just certificates. Longer term, I want to specialize in a focused area of cybersecurity where I can combine my software engineering background with security expertise, and contribute meaningfully to protecting systems and the people who rely on them.

I am motivated, methodical, and genuinely curious about how systems break and how to stop that from happening. This portfolio documents that ongoing journey.
